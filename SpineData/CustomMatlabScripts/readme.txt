CustomMatlabScripts: MATLAB scripts written exclusively for this spine study. 

findMinimums.m - Determines and visualizes the superior/inferior face.

getAttMat.m - Determines the transformation matrices given XYZ/AER Polhemus data.

normData.m - Normalizes sac and dist data for a given technique.

plotCalib.m - Plots the coordinate systems of beads and vertebrae.

plotScene.m - Plots polhemus, beads, and vertbrae.
